Free and Premium

AquaPlay IPTV for Android TV and Google TV · version 1.0.51

Everyday watching with one playlist is free and complete. Premium adds the power and the convenience: one Premium unlocks everything below, with a 7-day free trial. On a Samsung TV, AquaPlay is free with everything included.

Free

Playlists and setup

  • One playlist: M3U, Xtream Codes or MAG/Stalker portal
  • Add it from your phone by scanning a QR code, or type it in with the remote
  • Reload the playlist, and clear cached data for a fresh download
  • Clear messages when something fails, saying what went wrong and what to do

Live TV

  • Channel list with groups, search, favourites and Recently watched
  • Sort channels (provider order, by number, A to Z) and sort groups
  • Favourite groups, pin categories to the top, and set the group order
  • Hide channels and groups you don't watch, and hide empty groups
  • Channel logos, channel numbers in the list, alternating row colours
  • Type a channel number to jump to it; 0 for the last channel
  • Channel up/down while watching full screen, with your choice of which way Up goes
  • Resume the last channel when the app opens, in the panel or full screen
  • Hold OK for a menu while watching: audio, subtitles, Start over, schedule, catch-up guide, reload channel
  • Reconnects a dropped stream and reloads a frozen channel by itself
  • Data saver: holds channels that come in several qualities to a lower one

TV guide and catch-up

  • One guide (EPG) of your own, or the one from your playlist, refreshed daily
  • Now and next on the info bar, the channel's schedule beside the preview, and the full guide grid
  • Programme descriptions with pictures, adjustable size and auto-scrolling
  • TV Catalogue for today and yesterday
  • Catch-up for the last day: catch-up browser, Start over, rewind live TV
  • Catch-up channels marked with a clock
  • Guide time offset, for a guide whose times are wrong

Movies and Series

  • The whole Movies and Series library, with posters, ratings and stories
  • Continue watching: resume a film or episode where you stopped
  • Episodes and seasons, with watched marks; mark watched or not watched; hide watched
  • Up next: the next episode starts by itself, with a countdown you can set
  • Skip intros and credits, where TheIntroDB knows them (experimental)
  • Sync what you watch to your Simkl account
  • Hide films and series, and show or hide categories
  • Poster size, poster quality, or plain tiles with no posters
  • Movies and Series together or as separate tiles on the home screen
  • Playback speed for films and episodes
  • Built-in film engine (Nova) for hard-to-play files such as MKV

Playback and subtitles

  • Choice of audio and subtitle track, picture size, buffer and stream format
  • Preferred subtitle language, and subtitles turned on by themselves when the sound is in another language
  • Subtitle sync
  • Basic subtitle styles: size, 4 colours, dark box or outline, 3 heights
  • Play/Pause goes back to the picture from any screen
  • Screensaver, so a still picture doesn't burn in

Look and controls

  • Aqua theme, text size, and several channel screen layouts
  • Clock on screen while watching, its position and format; date format; info bar duration
  • Choose the start screen: Live TV or Movies and Series
  • 16 languages, including Hebrew and Arabic (right to left)
  • Works with the remote alone: arrows, OK and Back do everything
  • Settings pages with search
  • Share my settings with someone helping you (no passwords or addresses included)
  • 7-day free trial of every Premium feature

Premium

$5 a month, $10 a year, or $25 once, through Google Play

  • More than one playlist, and sending a playlist to another TV
  • Catch-up further back than one day (as far back as your provider keeps)
  • TV Catalogue beyond today and yesterday, and programme reminders
  • More than one guide, read together
  • Multi-view: up to four channels at once
  • Recording, including scheduling a programme from the guide
  • Profiles, each with their own favourites and history
  • Parental PIN, locked channels and locked groups, and adult channels hidden behind a PIN
  • Your own channel numbers, moving channels, and custom remote buttons
  • Your own channel groups: add channels, rename and delete groups
  • Sleep timer and stream delay warning
  • OLED and Custom themes (accent colours, backgrounds, brightness)
  • Subtitles found online (OpenSubtitles) and loaded automatically
  • More subtitle styles (12 more colours, any height, box and outline together, opacity, regular weight) and subtitles sent from your phone
  • Backup and restore, to your phone or a file

AquaPlay does not provide any channels or content; you need your own playlist from your provider. Prices are shown in your own currency in Google Play.

Some features that are free today may become part of Premium in the future.
